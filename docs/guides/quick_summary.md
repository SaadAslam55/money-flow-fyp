# Money Flow - System Summary

## 🎯 Purpose
Money Flow is a comprehensive **Business Operating System** designed to unify financial management, inventory control, and team collaboration into a single, real-time platform. Its primary purpose is to replace disjointed tools (spreadsheets, separate invoicing apps, inventory logbooks) with a centralized "Source of Truth" for all business activities.

## 👥 Who Can Use This?
This software is built for **Small to Medium Enterprises (SMEs)** across various industries:

*   **Retailers & Wholesalers:** For tracking stock levels, managing low-inventory alerts, and processing point-of-sale transactions.
*   **Service Providers:** For generating professional invoices, tracking payment statuses, and managing client relationships (CRM).
*   **Business Owners & Admins:** To view real-time dashboards, manage team permissions, and oversee organization health.
*   **Accountants & Bookkeepers:** To access financial reports, track expenses, and ensure tax compliance (GST/VAT/Sales Tax).
*   **Developers:** Who need to build custom integrations using the built-in API key and Webhook system.

## 🚀 Difference from Others
Money Flow stands out from traditional ERPs and simple accounting apps through:

1.  **Developer-First Extensibility:** Unlike closed systems, Money Flow offers **API Keys and Webhooks** out of the box, allowing businesses to connect with their own websites, custom apps, or third-party services effortlessly.
2.  **Granular Access Control:** Sophisticated **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** ensures cashiers can sell but not delete, accountants can view reports but not change inventory, and admins retain full control.
3.  **Real-Time Sync:** Built on **Supabase**, data updates instantly across all devices. If a cashier sells an item, the stock level updates immediately for the warehouse manager.
4.  **Integrated CRM & Communication:** Native support for **Email and SMS integrations** (SendGrid, Twilio, etc.) allows automated invoice delivery and payment reminders without leaving the app.
5.  **Modern & Fast:** A lightweight, responsive interface that works smoothly on any device, avoiding the bloat and slowness of legacy legacy enterprise software.

## 📈 How It Ensures Business Success
Money Flow drives success by transforming data into actionable intelligence:

*   **Prevents Revenue Loss:** Automated **Low Stock Alerts** (like the one for the Electric Stove) ensure you never run out of best-selling items.
*   **Accelerates Cash Flow:** Automated invoice tracking and professional payment reminders reduce overdue accounts and improve liquidity.
*   **Reduces Operational Costs:** By unifying Inventory, Finance, and HR/Team management, businesses save money on multiple software subscriptions.
*   **Ensures Compliance:** Flexible **Tax Settings** (inclusive/exclusive, compound taxes) ensure accurate financial reporting and reduced liability risks.
*   **Data-Driven Decisions:** comprehensive reporting provides visibility into top-selling products, peak sales times, and expense trends, allowing owners to pivot strategies quickly.
